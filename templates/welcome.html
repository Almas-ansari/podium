What gets looked at

The thinking
Did they give a real example or stay vague? Back a claim with a reason? Explore one idea properly, or touch five and drop them? Seven things are scored here, and this is the half that makes a mind sharper.
The speaking
Pace, pauses, filler words, repetition and how the voice moves. Measured from the audio and the word timings, never guessed, so the same recording always gives the same numbers.
Measured against {{ child.name }}
A young child's voice is naturally higher and quieter. Pitch and volume are judged against {{ child.name }}'s own first three speeches, never a fixed chart, so nobody is told they are shrieking for sounding their age.
