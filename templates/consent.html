What happens to the recording

It is transcribed, then deleted
The recording is sent to a speech-to-text service to produce a transcript, then analysed for pace, pauses and tone. It is deleted from our server the moment processing finishes. A copy stays in this browser on your own device so you can play it back; it never leaves your computer.
Only words and numbers are kept
The transcript and the measurements are stored on our side so you can see progress over time on the dashboard. A first name and an age band is all we hold about your child.
Two third parties, both named
Google, for your sign-in only — it receives your name, email and picture, and is told nothing about your child. Groq, for transcription. No advertising, no trackers, no analytics.
You can delete all of it
One button on the dashboard removes every recording, transcript, measurement and consent record. Your child is never shown a score, mark or percentage.

Your name and the date and time of this consent are stored with {{ child.name }}'s record.
