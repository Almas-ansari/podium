{# ------------------------------------------------------- feature: ideas #}

The half most apps skip

It scores the thinking, not just the talking

Anyone can count filler words. The harder question is whether a child said anything worth hearing — and that is the part that actually makes a mind sharper.

  • Did they give a concrete example, or stay vague?
  • Did they back a claim with a reason, or assert and drop it?
  • Did they explore one idea properly, or touch five and abandon them?
  • Did they find their own angle, or take the obvious one?

Idea dimensions, out of 5

{# ---------------------------------------------------- feature: delivery #}

Measured, never guessed

Every number comes from the audio

Pace, pauses, filler words and loudness are computed from the waveform and the word timings. No language model is asked how someone sounded, because it cannot hear them. Run the same recording twice and you get the same numbers.

  • Where the pauses cluster — three in the first fifteen seconds means a weak opening
  • Whether the voice fades in the final third, which one instruction usually fixes
  • Pitch and volume judged against your own child's baseline, never a fixed chart

{# ------------------------------------------------------------- principles #}

Built on three decisions

No scores shown to a child
A child who sees 43 out of 100 stops speaking. The encouraging feedback comes first; the numbers sit on a second tab, for a grown-up to open.
Exactly one thing to fix
Never a list. A child given five corrections fixes none of them, so the highest-impact one is chosen by a fixed priority order and the rest wait their turn.
Recordings stay on your device
Audio is deleted from the server the moment it has been processed. A first name and an age band is all we keep about a child.
{# ------------------------------------------------------------------- faq #}

Questions parents ask

Does my child need an account?

No. The account is yours. You add a profile per child, and they tap their name to practise. Children hold no password and give no email address.

What happens to the recording?

It is transcribed and analysed, then deleted from the server immediately. A playback copy stays in your own browser on your own device. We keep the transcript and the measurements, so the dashboard can show progress over time.

Will it tell my seven year old they sound wrong?

No. A young child's voice is naturally higher and quieter, so pitch and volume are judged against that child's own first three speeches rather than a fixed threshold. Until those exist, both are marked as calibrating and generate no feedback at all.

Is it useful for elocution and debate competitions?

That is what prepared mode is for. The five-box scaffold — opening line, three points, closing line — teaches speech structure, and the notes disappear when the timer starts, exactly as they would on stage.

How accurate is the transcript?

Automatic speech recognition makes mistakes, particularly with names and Indian-language words. The idea scoring is explicitly told to judge the thinking rather than the transcription, and every delivery number comes from the audio itself, not the text.
